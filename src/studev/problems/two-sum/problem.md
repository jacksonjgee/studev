Find the two positions whose numbers add up to the target.

## Input
- Line 1: the numbers, separated by spaces
- Line 2: the target

## Output
The two positions (from 0), smallest first, separated by a space.

## Example
```
in:  3 8 1 6
     7
out: 2 3
```

## Notes
- 2 to 10,000 numbers. There is always exactly one answer.
- Hint: can you do it in one pass with a dictionary?
